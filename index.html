项目背景Context

月底的账龄表，不该靠手工透视Month-end aging should not be manual pivot work

这是一个装在 Odoo 里的分析模块，而不是一个脱离业务系统的 Excel 模板。An analysis module inside Odoo, not an Excel template detached from the business system.

01

痛点Problem

月底手工把应收应付明细按到期日分段，容易算错；逾期客户靠 Excel 排序筛选，口径不统一，也难以追溯源文件。At month-end, AR/AP lines are bucketed by due date by hand. Errors creep in, overdue customers are sorted in Excel, definitions drift and the source file is hard to trace.

02

做法Approach

导入明细后自动清洗、按截止日分段、计算逾期天数与风险等级；看板、清单和导出共用同一套计算结果。Imported lines are cleaned, bucketed against the cutoff date, and scored for overdue days and risk. Dashboard, lists and exports all read the same computed result.

03

结果Outcome

111 行样例三方一致：Odoo 内计算结果、openpyxl 独立重算、预期值全部吻合；216 项验证通过、0 失败。111 sample lines agree three ways: Odoo's computed values, an independent openpyxl recalculation and the expected values. 216 checks pass, 0 fail.

一份数据，三种出口：看板、清单、报告。One dataset, three outputs: dashboard, list, report. 计算只做一次，展示和导出都引用同一结果，避免口径分裂。Compute once; display and export reference the same result, so definitions cannot drift.

处理链路Processing chain

从 Excel 到报告，六步Six steps from Excel to report

01

导入明细Import

xlsx / xls / csv，选择应收或应付。xlsx / xls / csv, tagged as receivable or payable.

02

识别与清洗Detect and clean

自动探测标题行，清洗日期与金额，推算到期日。Detect the header row, clean dates and amounts, derive due dates.

03

账龄分段Bucket

按“截止日 − 到期日”落入五段账龄。Bucket by cutoff date minus due date into five ranges.

04

风险分级Score risk

大额逾期与超长逾期分别标记高、中风险。Large and long-overdue balances are flagged high or watch.

05

看板与清单View

KPI、图表、TOP10 与逐行风险清单。KPIs, charts, top 10 and a line-level risk list.

06

导出报告Export

Excel 五表、风险清单、两页 PDF。Five-sheet Excel, risk list and a two-page PDF.

功能范围Scope

分析、清单、导出，共用一套口径Analysis, lists and exports share one definition

01

中文表头自动识别Header detection

xlsx / xls / csv 自动探测标题行，日期与金额清洗，缺到期日时自动推算，并自动创建往来单位。xlsx / xls / csv with automatic header detection, date and amount cleaning, due-date derivation and automatic partner creation.

02

五段账龄Five aging buckets

未到期、0–30、30–60、60–90、90 天以上，按截止日与到期日之差计算。Not due, 0–30, 30–60, 60–90 and 90+ days, computed from cutoff date minus due date.

03

逾期风险清单Overdue risk list

大额逾期 ≥ 10 万标红为高风险；逾期超过 90 天或余额 ≥ 5 万标黄为关注。Overdue ≥ 100k is high risk in red; over 90 days or balance ≥ 50k is watch in amber.

04

可视化看板Dashboard

OWL 2 + Chart.js 4，含 KPI、账龄分布、逾期占比、TOP10 欠款客户和风险清单。OWL 2 and Chart.js 4 with KPIs, aging distribution, overdue share, top 10 debtors and the risk list.

05

多格式导出Multiple exports

Excel 五表报告、风险清单 Excel、A4 两页 PDF，以及可复用的导入模板。A five-sheet Excel report, a risk-list Excel, a two-page A4 PDF and a reusable import template.

06

参数与留痕Settings and traceability

大额阈值可配置；批次记录源文件、导入人、时间与汇总，便于回溯。The large-amount threshold is configurable; each batch records the source file, importer, time and summary.

真实性边界Scope and honesty

往来单位、金额、发票号与日期均为合成数据；演示账号与本地数据库口令仅用于本机环境。Partners, amounts, invoice numbers and dates are synthetic. The demo login and local database credentials are for the local machine only.
